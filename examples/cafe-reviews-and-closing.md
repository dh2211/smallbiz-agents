# Café: review replies and a closing checklist

> **Owner request.** I run Corner Cup, a small café (invented). Two reviews came in this week. Draft replies, signed "Mai, Corner Cup". Then write up how we close the café at night, from my notes, and make a checklist for the wall.
>
> Review 1, 5 stars: "Best flat white in the neighbourhood and the banana bread is still warm at 8am. Lovely staff."
>
> Review 2, 2 stars: "Waited 25 minutes for two coffees on Saturday. Nobody said anything. Won't rush back."
>
> What happened Saturday: one barista was off sick. From this weekend we have two baristas on Saturday mornings. I'm happy to offer a free coffee next visit.
>
> Closing notes: last orders 5:30, close 6. Wipe and backflush the machine. Empty grinders into airtight tubs. Fridge temps written on the sheet. Leftover dairy dated. Cash up, float 100 in the safe. Floors. Bins out back. Alarm. Lights.

## Closing SOP (`biz-sop-writer`)

**Purpose.** Close the café so it is clean, safe, and ready for an early opening.

**Who.** Closing barista. **When.** Daily, 5:30 to about 6:30 pm. **Takes.** About 60 minutes.

**You need.** Machine cleaning tablets, blind basket, airtight tubs, temperature sheet, date labels, safe key, alarm code (ask the owner; never write it down here).

**Steps**

1. At 5:30, call last orders.
2. At 6:00, lock the front door and turn the sign.
3. Backflush the coffee machine with a cleaning tablet and wipe the steam wands and drip tray.
4. Empty the grinders into airtight tubs.
5. **Safety check.** Write both fridge temperatures on the temperature sheet. `[owner to confirm the safe range against your local food-safety rules]`
6. **Safety check.** Date-label all open dairy. Throw away anything past its use-by. `[owner to confirm how long opened dairy is kept]`
7. Cash up. Put the float of 100 in the safe and the rest in the bank bag.
8. Sweep and mop the floors.
9. Take the bins out the back.
10. Turn off the lights, set the alarm, and lock the back door.

**What good looks like.** Temperature sheet filled in for today. Float counts to 100. No undated dairy in the fridge.

**If something goes wrong.** If a fridge reads outside the safe range, call the owner before you leave. If the alarm won't set, call `[alarm company number]`.

**Open questions.** Who checks the temperature sheet, and how often? Where does the bank bag go overnight?
