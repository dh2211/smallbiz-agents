# Use the skills

After you [install the skills](install.md), ask your agent for what you need in plain English. The agent matches your request to a skill's description and loads it. You can also name the skill: "Use the biz-quote-builder skill to..."

## Brief it like a colleague

The skills ask for missing details, but a complete first request saves a round trip. Give the facts the AI can't know:

- what you sell or do, with real specs, sizes, and materials,
- your prices, discounts, tax rate, and terms, exactly as you charge them,
- your tone, with an example if you have one ("friendly, a bit cheeky"),
- where it's going: shop window, website, a named marketplace, email, or text message.

Leave out customer names, addresses, order numbers, and payment details.

## Requests that work well

- "Write a website description for our 250 ml lavender hand cream: shea butter, no added colour, 8.50. Friendly tone."
- "Here's a 2-star review about slow service on Saturday. We've added a second barista on weekends. Draft a reply."
- "Write an SOP for cashing up the till at closing, from my notes below."
- "Turn that SOP into a one-page closing checklist."
- "Quote for 12 hand-tied bouquets at 45 each plus delivery 20. Prices include tax. Valid 14 days."
- "Plan a two-week back-to-school promotion: 10% off backpacks, budget 150 for flyers, we post on Instagram and our email list."
- "Text message telling a customer their order is delayed to Friday."

## Bigger jobs

Ask for the `biz-shopfront-agent` workflow, or say: "Write descriptions for our three new mugs, then plan a launch week and its posts." The agent works through the steps and stops after each one for you to approve or change it.

## Check everything before you use it

1. Check every price, quantity, discount, tax line, total, and term. Redo the totals yourself.
2. Search for `[owner to confirm]` and fill in or remove each one.
3. Check every claim about a product or offer is true, and any "limited" or deadline wording is real.
4. Check SOP safety, hygiene, and legal steps against the rules that apply to your business.
5. Read every reply and message before you send or post it. Nothing goes out automatically.

## Keep customer data out

Use placeholders such as `[customer first name]` and `[order reference]`, and add the real details in your shop system or email. The skills remove details they see, but by then the text has reached the AI provider.
