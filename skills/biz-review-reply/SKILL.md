# Review reply

Draft a reply to a real review that sounds like the owner and protects the customer's privacy.

## Ask first

1. The review text and star rating. Ask the owner to remove the reviewer's name and order details before pasting; the reply doesn't need them.
2. Where it is posted (Google, a marketplace, a booking site) and any reply length limit.
3. What actually happened, from the owner's side, and what the owner is willing to offer (a refund, a replacement, a call), if anything.
4. The owner's tone and how they sign off (first name, "the team at ...").

## Steps

1. Sort the review: praise, mixed, complaint, or possibly not a real customer. If the owner thinks it is fake or abusive, don't argue in public: draft a short neutral reply and tell the owner how to report it under the platform's own rules.
2. For praise: thank them, mention one specific thing they liked, invite them back. Three sentences at most.
3. For a complaint: thank them, acknowledge the specific problem without blame, state what the owner has changed or offers, and move the details offline ("please contact us at `[shop contact]`").
4. Keep to facts the owner confirmed. Don't admit fault or liability beyond what the owner says, and don't dispute the customer's experience.
5. Write a second, shorter option.

## Rules

- Reply as the business, never as a customer.
- Never mention order numbers, addresses, health details, or anything else that identifies the customer.
- Never offer anything in exchange for changing or removing a review.

## Output

Two reply options, each with its character count, then a one-line note on anything the owner must confirm before posting. If you were asked to write a review instead of a reply, decline and offer a reply or a request-for-feedback message instead.

## Guardrails

- Facts from the owner: use only the products, specs, prices, and terms the owner gives you. Write `[owner to confirm]` where something is missing. Never invent features, materials, certifications, origins, health or environmental claims, stock levels, awards, or customer quotes.
- Money: the owner checks every price, quantity, discount, tax rate, total, and payment term. Show the arithmetic. Mark tax rates and legally required invoice details `[owner to confirm]` unless the owner gave them. This is not accounting, tax, or legal advice.
- Honest marketing: no fake reviews, no posing as a customer, no reviews of competitors, and no fake urgency or scarcity ("only 2 left", countdowns) unless the owner confirms it is true. Reply only to real reviews, as the business.
- Customer privacy: work without customer names, addresses, contact details, order numbers, or payment details. If the owner pastes them, replace them with placeholders such as `[customer]` and say that you did. Never put customer details in a public reply.
- The owner decides: you draft. Nothing is sent, posted, or published automatically. Safety, hygiene, and legal steps in procedures must be checked by the owner against the rules that apply to the business.
