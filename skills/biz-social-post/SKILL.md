# Social post

Write posts that sound like the owner, say something true, and fit the platform.

## Ask first

1. What the post is about, with the facts: product, offer with exact dates, event time and place, or change in hours.
2. The platform and format: feed post, story, short video caption, community group.
3. The owner's voice, with one or two past posts if they have them.
4. Whether there's a photo or video, and what it shows.

## Steps

1. Open with the most useful fact for a customer, in the first line.
2. Add one or two sentences of detail, in the owner's voice.
3. End with one clear next step: visit, reply, order, book. Use the owner's real link or address placeholder `[link]`.
4. Add three to five plain, relevant hashtags if the platform uses them. No trending tags unrelated to the post.
5. Suggest an image or short video idea the owner can shoot on a phone.
6. Write a second version with a different opening.

## Rules

- No fake giveaways, no "comment to win" unless the owner runs a real one with rules, and no engagement bait.
- If the post is paid for or features a partner, say so plainly and follow the platform's disclosure rules.

## Output

Two versions, each with character count, hashtags, and an image idea. For "a week of posts", a table with day, topic, post text, and image idea.

## Guardrails

- Facts from the owner: use only the products, specs, prices, and terms the owner gives you. Write `[owner to confirm]` where something is missing. Never invent features, materials, certifications, origins, health or environmental claims, stock levels, awards, or customer quotes.
- Money: the owner checks every price, quantity, discount, tax rate, total, and payment term. Show the arithmetic. Mark tax rates and legally required invoice details `[owner to confirm]` unless the owner gave them. This is not accounting, tax, or legal advice.
- Honest marketing: no fake reviews, no posing as a customer, no reviews of competitors, and no fake urgency or scarcity ("only 2 left", countdowns) unless the owner confirms it is true. Reply only to real reviews, as the business.
- Customer privacy: work without customer names, addresses, contact details, order numbers, or payment details. If the owner pastes them, replace them with placeholders such as `[customer]` and say that you did. Never put customer details in a public reply.
- The owner decides: you draft. Nothing is sent, posted, or published automatically. Safety, hygiene, and legal steps in procedures must be checked by the owner against the rules that apply to the business.
